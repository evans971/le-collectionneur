Valmont: Last Door Out — Privacy Statement

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This statement explains what data the game Valmont: Last Door Out handles when you play online (private rooms, public matchmaking and the online leaderboard). Solo play works without sending any data to our server.

What we collect

  • Nickname you choose (or a random one such as “Player1234”). It is shown to other players and on the leaderboard. Offensive nicknames are filtered.
  • A random device identifier generated by the game and stored in your browser, used only to keep your best score on the leaderboard.
  • Game data: scores, day reached, chosen character, and live game state during a multiplayer session (position, progress, sabotages).

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts or payment information. We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising IDs or third-party analytics.

How it is used

Only to run multiplayer sessions and to display the leaderboard. Live game state is kept in memory for the duration of a session and is not stored. Leaderboard entries (nickname, score, day, character, date) are stored on our server.

Where it is stored

Our game server is hosted by Render (render.com) and leaderboard data is stored in a Neon (neon.tech) PostgreSQL database. Data is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Local storage

The game saves your settings, progress, coins and achievements in your browser's local storage. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser data.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information and does not allow free-text chat. Nicknames are filtered for offensive words.

Your rights and contact

To ask for your leaderboard entry to be deleted, contact the developer through the game's page on Poki or itch.io and give your nickname.
