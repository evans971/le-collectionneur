        Valmont.

        Last Door Out

        Nine prisoners, nine wings, five days. Find the shards, hide from the hunters, sabotage the others. Only one door leads out.

        Nickname: — · Coins: 0

        How to play

        The goal

        Each day, find the terminals in your wing and solve their puzzles to get code shards. With all the shards, the airlock door opens.

        The last player out of their wing is eliminated every night. On day 5, only the first one through the door is free.

        If everyone else finishes before you, you lose. Watch the rivals' bars at the top right.

        Clues

        Some codes and protocols are written on notes in other rooms. They stay in your notebook J. The files tell you why you are here.

        Hide and sneak

        Hunters see in a cone in front of them and hear your steps. Walking makes noise, running a lot, crouching almost none.

        Crouched behind furniture (table, crate, bed, piano…), you become invisible to a hunter on the other side, even if he is chasing you.

        Hide in lockers and under beds E, but not while they are watching. Air vents connect two rooms.

        At night, switch off your flashlight F to be less visible. Cameras set off the alarm.

        When chasing, a hunter is faster than you walking: run, break his line of sight, hide or take a vent. Caught? Back to your cell, then 5 s of protection when you come out. Three captures in one day and you are eliminated.

        Sabotage

        On the first day there is only one hunter and nobody can send more. After that, extra hunters become a penalty.

        Each shard earns 2 tokens. Spend them R to slow the others down: power cut, locked doors, gas, hunter…

        The others do the same to you. A jammer automatically blocks the next penalty. A spy drone breaks if you stay right under it for 5 s: it backs away, so follow it.

        Once seen, terminals and the exit door stay marked on the map at the top right.

        Rivals can offer an alliance over the walkie-talkie. It might be sincere.

        Keys (PC)

        ZQSD/arrows move · Shift run · C crouch · E action · Space ability · 1 decoy · 2 smoke bomb · F flashlight · R sabotage · J notebook · H collapse panel · Esc pause. The mouse aims the flashlight. You can change the keys in Settings.

        Controls (mobile)

        Put your thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen: a joystick appears. Your flashlight follows your direction. On the right: Action (search, hide, puzzles, door), Run (hold), Crouch, Light, Ability, Decoy, Smoke bomb, Sabotage. Holding your phone sideways gives you a wider view.
